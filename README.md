# 📊 Excel Retail Sales Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Retail Sales Dashboard built in Microsoft Excel to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer segments, and regional trends. The dashboard enables business users to explore key performance indicators (KPIs) and make data-driven decisions.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze retail sales performance.
- Track key business KPIs.
- Identify high-performing regions and categories.
- Discover the most profitable customer segments.
- Monitor monthly sales trends.
- Present business insights through an interactive dashboard.

---

## 🛠 Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Slicers
- Timeline
- Conditional Formatting

---

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- 💰 Total Sales
- 💵 Total Profit
- 📦 Total Orders
- 🛒 Average Order Value (AOV)

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

- Sales by Region
- Sales by Category
- Profit by Customer Segment
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Top 10 Products
- Interactive Slicers and Timeline

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- The South region generated the highest sales.
- The Consumer segment produced the highest profit.
- Technology was the best-performing product category.
- A small number of products generated a large share of total sales.
- Monthly sales remained relatively stable with seasonal fluctuations.
